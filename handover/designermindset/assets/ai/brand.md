# Designer Mindset brand

> Designer Mindset helps designers turn a design skill into a business.

- **Who it's for:** The freelancer without a system. Around 38, four years in, feast or famine. Charges day rates, knows they're undercharging, and doesn't know how to say a bigger number. Already has clients, so the pain is weekly.
- **The promise:** Learn the business side once in the Six-Figure Designer programme, then run it every week in Agency Operator, next to other designers.
- **Proof:** 1,000+ designers in the community. Every lesson comes from 25 years of running a design business, not from a course template.
- **Sits next to:** Cursor for short, declarative copy. 37signals for practitioners teaching business with opinions. Linear for tool-grade precision.
- **Against:** Guru course sites (big face, yellow highlighter, countdown timers). Agency templates ("we craft digital experiences"). SaaS defaults (purple gradients, glassy cards, "AI-powered" badges).

Live guidelines and every asset: https://crucial.design/handover/designermindset

## Logo

The lockup puts the symbol and the name side by side as one signature. Keep the proportions and the gap as supplied at every size. Below 24px the name gets hard to read, so use the symbol on its own for avatars, favicons and anything small.

- On light backgrounds: Use the Ink lockup on White, Soft and other light backgrounds. This is the default for documents, the site header and anything printed in one colour.
- On dark backgrounds: Use the White lockup on Ink and Orange. Keep the lockup in one colour so the symbol and the name read together. Orange on Ink is for the symbol alone, as an accent, never the full lockup.
- Clear space: Keep clear space of x on every side, where x is half the height of the symbol. Nothing goes inside it: no text, no edge of the screen, no other logo. On a busy page, give it more.
- Minimum size: The lockup stays at 24px tall or more on screen (12mm in print). Below that, switch to the symbol, which holds down to 16px.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour, add effects to, or place the logo on a busy photo or a low-contrast field. Use the supplied files as they are. If a layout seems to need a change to the logo, change the layout.
- Files: https://crucial.design/handover/designermindset/assets/marks/ (symbol-*.svg, lockup-*.svg)

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Token | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orange | #FF4500 | `--accent` | The brand colour. Primary buttons, kickers, highlights, the one thing on the page you should notice first. |
| Ink | #0A0A0A | `--dark` | Dark blocks, hero sections, secondary buttons and body text on light. |
| White | #FFFFFF | `--page` | The page. Always the page. Dark is a block on white, never the page itself. |
| Soft | #F5F5F5 | `--soft` | Cards and panels on white. Depth comes from this contrast, never from shadows. |
| Muted | #7D7D7D | `--muted` | Secondary text on light. Use at 18px and up, see readable combinations. |

Orange is the signal. It earns attention because there is so little of it: one primary button, a kicker, a highlight. If two things on a page are orange, one of them is wrong.
Ink and White do the work. Build each page as white with near-black rounded blocks for the moments that matter: the hero, the call to action, a feature. Soft gives cards their edge without a border or a shadow.

Proportions: Orange 10%, Ink 30%, White 45%, Soft 10%, Muted 5%

Contrast: Ink on White and White on Ink pass everything. Orange is a large-text and button colour: white on orange holds for 16px semibold button labels and anything 24px and up, but never for body copy. Muted on White falls just short of AA for small text, so keep it to 18px ledes and captions people don't need to read.

## Typography

- **Instrument Serif** (400): One serif moment per page: the H1. It carries the voice. Let it have room, and never set a section heading or a button in it.
- **Instrument Sans** (600 · 700): Everything structural: section headings, card titles and the wordmark. 700 for H2, 600 for H3 and the name in the lockup. Tight tracking, -0.015em.
- **Inter** (400 · 500 · 600): Reading. Body at 16px, line height 1.6, ledes at 17 to 18px in Muted. 600 for kickers, sentence case, in Orange.

| Style | Font | Weight | Size | Line height | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display | Instrument Serif | 400 | 56px | 1.08 | -0.015em |
| Heading 1 | Instrument Serif | 400 | 44px | 1.08 | -0.015em |
| Heading 2 | Instrument Sans | 700 | 40px | 1.1 | -0.015em |
| Heading 3 | Instrument Sans | 600 | 22px | 1.25 | -0.01em |
| Lede | Inter | 400 | 18px | 1.6 | 0 |

Mobile drops Display to 36px and Heading 2 to 30px. Everything else holds. The jump from serif headline to sans structure does the work, so the scale itself stays quiet.

## Voice

Designer Mindset sounds like a designer who has run the business side for 25 years and still remembers not knowing how. Direct, warm, a bit cheeky. Plain truths, never promises. The reader has been burned by course sellers before, and one whiff of guru undoes everything else.

### Say the plain truth
Name the gap. Don't sell the dream.

Say: "You are good at design. The business side is the gap." / "Most designers undercharge. Here's why." / "I priced my first retainer too low too."
Avoid: "Unlock your creative potential." / "Transform your design career today." / "The ultimate blueprint for success."

### A practitioner, never a guru
Show the work, the desk, the numbers. No hype.

Say: "Here's the proposal that won the retainer." / "This is what I'd charge for that." / "Ask it in the community, someone has been there."
Avoid: "6 figures in 90 days." / "My students made $X last month!" / "Only 3 spots left, offer ends tonight."

### Short, sentence case, human
Sentence case everywhere. No em dashes. Say it in fewer words.

Say: "Join Free" / "Mastery Membership" / "See inside"
Avoid: "JOIN NOW" / "Ready to supercharge your journey?" / "Leverage our ecosystem of resources."

### Product copy

| Where | Say | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Buttons | Join Free · See inside · Book a call | Submit · Click here · Get started now! |
| Errors | That email is already in. Log in instead? | Error 409: user exists. |
| Empty states | No posts yet. Ask the first question, someone has been there. | Nothing to see here! |
| Success | You're in. Your first call is Thursday. | Congratulations!!! 🎉 Welcome aboard! |
| Emails | Subject: Your pricing call is tomorrow | Subject: Don't miss out!!! |

## Imagery

The person is the product, so real photos of Oykun are part of the system. Always a practitioner at work: the desk, the laptop, the whiteboard, a live call. Real screenshots of the community and the tools do the rest.

Do: Practitioner at work: desk, laptop, sketch, whiteboard, call; Natural light and real places; Real screenshots of the community, the programme and Agency Operator; Real member avatars, with permission

Don't: Big face with yellow text on top; Pointing at the camera or at a number; Luxury props: cars, watches, beaches, cash; Stock photos of people in meetings; Decorative illustration or AI-generated scenes

## Interface

The interface is built from the same tokens as the brand. White page, rounded dark blocks for the moments that matter, borderless soft cards and pill buttons. Depth comes from contrast and rounding, never from shadows.

- **Buttons on light:** Pills, always. Primary is Orange, secondary is Ink. 20/48 padding on desktop, 16/32 on mobile, Inter 600 at 16px. One primary per view.
- **Buttons on dark:** Primary stays Orange. Secondary turns transparent with a 25% white border.
- **Card:** White inside a soft section, Soft on a white page. 24px radius, 32px padding, no border, no shadow.
- **Tags and status:** Pills again. Shipped or active is Orange with white text. In progress or internal is white with Muted text.
- **Section heading:** Orange kicker in sentence case, then the heading in Instrument Sans 700, then a muted lede no wider than 660px.
- **Dark block:** The signature. Hero, call to action and feature moments sit in an Ink block with a 40px radius (60px wide, 28px mobile), inset from the screen edge so the rounding shows. Two dark blocks never touch.

Radius: Block 40px (60 on wide screens, 28 on mobile), Card 24px (Cards and inner panels), Inner 16px (Images and inputs inside cards), Pill full (Buttons, tags, avatars)

Spacing: 8px (Page inset, so blocks never touch the edge), 12px (Gap between cards), 24px (Inside small cards), 32px (Card padding), 64px (Section rhythm on mobile), 96px (Section rhythm on desktop)

Content caps at 1080px, text columns at 640 to 720px. Alternate white, dark block, white or soft. The footer stays plain white.

There is no dark mode. Dark is a block on a white page, never the page itself. If a platform forces a dark theme, use the White lockup and keep Orange for the one action.

## Social media

Use the approved headers with the symbol as the profile picture. Orange is the default avatar field; Ink and White are approved alternatives when a platform's own interface needs a different contrast. The name always sits next to the avatar on every platform, so the avatar is the symbol alone.

Platforms cover part of the header with the avatar and buttons: bottom left on X and LinkedIn, everything outside the centre on YouTube. The text sits in the safe area of each, so the files work as they are.

## Advertising

Ads use the same system as everything else: the white page, one dark block, one orange action and a plain sentence. No countdowns, no income claims, no faces with yellow text. If an ad would look at home on a guru's page, it's wrong.

- One idea per ad. One sentence in the image, the detail goes in the post text.
- The action is always a plain verb: Join, Learn more, Sign up.
- Real photos and real screenshots only. Oykun at work, the product as it is.
- Keep text out of the platform's safe zones: the top 250px and bottom 340px of stories, the bottom of feed images where the headline bar sits.
- No numbers you can't stand behind. "1,000+ designers" is true; "6 figures in 90 days" never is.

Examples:
- LinkedIn Single image (1200 × 627): image says "You are good at design. The business side is the gap."; post text "Most designers learn pricing, clients and positioning the hard way, alone. 1,000+ designers are learning it together instead."; action "Join"
- LinkedIn Square image (1200 × 1200): image says "I priced my first retainer too low too."; post text "25 years of running a design business, turned into a programme you work through with other designers."; action "Learn more"
- Instagram Feed 4:5 (1080 × 1350): image says "Monthly live Q&A. Bring your pricing question."; post text "Every month, live, with Oykun and the community. Recordings for every call."; action "Sign up"
- Instagram Story and Reels (1080 × 1920): image says "Stop figuring out the business side alone."; action "Join us. Free"
- X Promoted post (1200 × 675): image says "Most designers undercharge. Here's why."; post text "A free community for designers who want the business side to work as well as the design."; action "Join"

## Never

- No gradients, no glassmorphism, no shadows.
- No thin bordered cards. Cards are borderless soft blocks.
- No full dark mode. Dark is a block on a white page.
- No decorative illustration. Real screenshots and real photos only.
- No all-caps labels. Kickers are sentence case, Orange, 600.
- No square buttons, no outline orange buttons. Pills, always.
